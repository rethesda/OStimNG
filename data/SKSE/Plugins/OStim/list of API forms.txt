this is just a list of the general API forms

### Factions ###
	note: faction ranks are mirrors of the represented values, changing faction ranks will not change the actual values
	note: actors will only be in these factions during scenes

OStimExcitementFaction - 0xD93
	faction rank will reflect the current excitement level of the actor

OStimActorCountFaction - 0xECA
	faction rank will reflect the amount of actors in the scene

OStimTimesClimaxedFaction - 0xE49
	faction rank will reflect how many times the actor climaxed in the current scene

OStimTimeUntilClimaxFaction - 0xE4A
	faction rank will reflect the time in seconds until the actor reaches a climax at their current excitement rate
	a value of 101 means more than 100 seconds, a value of -1 means the actor cannot reach a climax in the current animation

OStimSchlongifiedFaction - 0xE9C
	faction membership will reflect whether or not the actor has a penis, mainly used to identify futas

OStimInWaterFaction - 0xEDF
	faction membership will reflect whether or not the scene takes place in water, mainly used to washing animations


### Formlists ###

OStimActorList - 0x815
	a list of all actors that have participated in an OStim scene at least once
